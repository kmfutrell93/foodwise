## Description

```
FoodWise is the nutrition companion built for people on GLP-1 medications — Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, and more.

Eating on a GLP-1 isn’t a normal diet. Appetite drops. Nausea spikes after your shot. Protein gets harder. FoodWise plans around that — not against it.

WHAT YOU GET

• Injection-day aware meal plans
  Soft, low-fat meals on shot day. Higher-fiber options mid-week when constipation tends to hit. Your plan rotates with your medication schedule.

• Medication-specific personalization
  Semaglutide and tirzepatide aren’t the same. FoodWise adjusts nausea profile, portion density, and protein emphasis to your drug.

• 100–130g protein targets
  Built for muscle preservation when appetite is low — smaller, protein-dense meals you can actually finish.

• Symptom tracker + AI insights
  Log nausea, fatigue, bloating, and more. After a few logs, Nori (your AI coach) surfaces patterns and food adjustments. Those insights feed your next meal plan.

• Grocery list under budget
  One list from your week’s meals, sectioned for the store, trimmed to your weekly budget.

• Meal swaps when something doesn’t sit right
  Swap a single meal without regenerating the whole week.

• Progress that matters
  Streaks, milestones, weight logging, and a weekly wellness report.

WHO IT’S FOR

People starting or escalating a GLP-1 who are tired of guessing what to eat — and tired of generic meal apps that ignore injection day entirely.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

FoodWise is a nutrition support tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace your doctor or dietitian. Always follow your prescribing provider’s guidance.

Start with a free trial of FoodWise Pro. Cancel anytime in Apple Subscriptions.
```
