FoodWise Support

Nutrition support for people on GLP-1 medications. We’re here to help — this page covers the questions we get most often.

How does meal plan generation work?

FoodWise builds a 7-day plan from your medication, injection day, appetite, restrictions, aversions, and budget. Injection day meals use softer, lower-fat options. After the plan is ready, a grocery list is built from meal ingredients and trimmed to stay near your weekly budget.

Is FoodWise medical advice?

No. FoodWise is a nutrition / wellness tool — not a medical device and not a substitute for your doctor or dietitian. Always follow your prescribing provider’s guidance for medication and diet.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FoodWise → Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period. Refunds are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the FoodWise paywall (or Settings → Subscription) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, meal plans, grocery data, symptom logs, streaks, milestones, weekly reports, weight logs, and auth record. It cannot be undone.

Why didn’t my meal plan finish generating?

Generation can take up to about a minute on cellular. Keep the app open on the generating screen. If it fails, tap Try again / New week. Check your connection and try on Wi‑Fi if LTE is unstable.

Notifications

If you opted in, FoodWise may send injection-day reminders, a weekly report nudge, and occasional re-engagement messages. Turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → FoodWise, or adjust options in the app Settings where available.

Still stuck? Email support@foodwise.app. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.
